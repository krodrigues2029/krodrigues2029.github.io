About Me

I have experience using Adobe Creative Cloud to develop graphics, social media content, videos, and visual brand identities. I am especially interested in how digital graphics shape the way sports organizations communicate with fans and build recognizable brands across social media.

My goal is to continue developing both my marketing and creative skills while applying them to projects involving sports, branding, social media, and digital communication.
